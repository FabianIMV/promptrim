Frequently Asked Questions

Does this work for any AI model?
Yes. Compressed prompts work with ChatGPT (GPT-4o, GPT-4.1), Claude (Sonnet, Haiku, Opus), Gemini, Mistral, and any LLM. Tokens are counted similarly across all major providers.
Is my data private?
In Fast Mode, compression happens 100% in your browser — nothing leaves your device. In AI Mode, your prompt is sent directly from your browser to Anthropic's API using your own API key. We never see your prompts or keys.
How much can I save?
Fast Mode typically saves 20-40%. AI Mode saves 40-70% on verbose prompts. Savings depend on how wordy the original prompt is — instructions with lots of filler compress the most.
Will compression change what the AI does?
Slight differences are possible, especially in Aggressive mode. Balanced mode is designed to preserve the full intent and context of your original prompt. Always review the output before using in production.
How is the token count estimated?
We use a character-based heuristic (1 token ≈ 4 characters for English) which is accurate within ~10% for most prompts. For exact counts, use your provider's tokenizer library.
